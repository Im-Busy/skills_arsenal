# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [0.2.0] - 2026-08-20

### Changed

- Restricted activation to requests that explicitly name or invoke
  `stop-slop-zh`; general Chinese rewriting routes to `deslop-zh`.
- Replaced absolute style mandates with meaning-preserving, contextual guidance.
- Added safeguards for facts, uncertainty, terminology, and sourced examples.

### Removed

- Removed automatic application to every Chinese response.
- Removed mandatory colloquial phrase counts, punctuation bans, fixed article
  structures, and supporting-detail requirements that could encourage
  fabrication.
- Removed legacy reference and promotional files that contradicted the new
  activation and safety policy.
